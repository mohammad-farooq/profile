FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions I get asked most often.

What services do you offer? +

I offer 12 core services covering web development, mobile apps, cloud, cybersecurity, AI, automation, data analytics, ERP/CRM, SEO, UI/UX design, and IT consulting. If you need something custom, just ask.

How much does a typical project cost? +

Costs depend on scope and complexity. My standard plans start at $299, $799, and $1,999. Enterprise solutions are quoted based on requirements. You'll always get a clear, fixed quote before we start.

How long does a project take? +

Landing pages can be done in 1–2 weeks. Full websites typically take 3–6 weeks. Complex software and cloud migrations range from 6 weeks to several months. You'll get a timeline upfront.

Do you design in Figma? +

Yes! All my UI/UX designs are created in Figma with reusable components, auto-layout, and interactive prototypes. You get full access to the Figma files at handoff.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Yes. I offer 24/7 support retainers with guaranteed response times, plus ad-hoc support for past clients. Many clients stay with me for years for maintenance and updates.

Do you work with international clients? +

Absolutely. I'm based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and work remotely with clients worldwide, adapting to your timezone. Payments are accepted in multiple currencies.
